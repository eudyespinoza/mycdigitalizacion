# Visual direction: Pulso Comercial

## Grounded direction roll

1. Marketplace noticeboard: dense offers organized as trusted neighborhood signals.
2. Product packaging system: crisp labels, modular category color and physical cutouts.
3. **Pulso Comercial**: search-led retail rhythm, rounded brand geometry, asymmetric product staging and direct conversion.
4. Postal logistics sheet: route, delivery and trust information as precise service notation.
5. Modern stationery counter: bright surface, tactile product groupings and quick category access.
6. Digital department store directory: strong wayfinding and varied merchandising scale.
7. Checkout progress ribbon: progressive disclosure with visible certainty at every step.

Impeccable assigned index 3 with seed `db399cd4`, matching the user's approved direction.

## Challenger rulings

- Futurist bolted book: declined on product clarity and audience identification. Kept discipline: more decisive type scale and compositional energy.
- Airline timetable slide rack: competitive on scanning clarity, weaker on brand warmth. Kept discipline: compact metadata and visible position/state.
- ANSI BBS: declined for mainstream commerce. Kept discipline: complete keyboard operation and honest system states.
- Oscilloscope: declined for audience identification. Kept discipline: precise, unambiguous status feedback.
- Mesophotic dive: declined for shopping clarity. Kept discipline: staged discovery from broad category to product detail.
- Accretion disk: declined for performance and familiarity. Kept discipline: a single dominant focal action per viewport.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS:** A broad catalog can feel immediate and trustworthy when search, categories and real products behave like one energetic retail pulse; refuse the centered generic hero and equal-card wall.
- **OWN-WORLD:** White and cold-white surfaces, deep navy structure, cyan wayfinding, magenta conversion, rounded logo geometry, crisp product cutouts and soft tinted shadows.
- **STORY:** Understand breadth, find a category or search, trust delivery/payment signals, inspect a product and advance without uncertainty.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT:** Slim trust rail, practical search-led header, left-aligned oversized promise, right-side product still life, magenta catalog action, compact trust facts and category rail entering the fold.
- **FORM:** Pulso Comercial, grounded direction 3, seed `db399cd4`.
- **FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
